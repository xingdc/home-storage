家图 · 隐私政策简体中文

生效日期：2026年5月22日

我们不收集任何个人信息

家图是一款完全离线的家庭物品管理应用。我们不做以下任何事：

  • 不收集您的姓名、邮箱、电话号码或任何个人身份信息
  • 不追踪您的位置
  • 不使用任何统计分析、广告或追踪 SDK
  • 不接入第三方登录服务
  • 不收集崩溃报告（除非您主动通过 App Store 反馈）

您的数据在哪里

您在家图中创建的全部数据——包括物品信息、房间布局、户型图、收纳照片、分类库——都仅存储在您的设备本地。

我们没有任何服务器，无法访问、查看或获取您的任何数据。

数据导出

家图支持您将全部数据导出为文件，存储到您的 iOS「文件」App 中。导出操作由您主动触发，数据文件完全在您的控制之下。

App 内购买

家图的付费扩展（收纳地图、楼层管理）通过 Apple App Store 内购完成。支付信息由 Apple 处理，我们无法获取您的付款信息。

未成年人

家图不收集任何人的个人信息，包括未成年人。

隐私政策更新

本政策如有更新，将在 App 更新时随附新版本。

联系我们

如有隐私相关问题，请联系：xingdachuan@outlook.com

Jiatu · Privacy PolicyEnglish

Effective Date: May 22, 2026

We Do Not Collect Any Personal Information

Jiatu is a fully offline home item management app. We do not:

  • Collect your name, email, phone number, or any personally identifiable information
  • Track your location
  • Use any analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs
  • Integrate any third-party login services
  • Collect crash reports (unless you submit feedback through the App Store)

Where Your Data Lives

All data you create in Jiatu — including item information, room layouts, floor plans, storage photos, and category libraries — is stored exclusively on your device locally.

We have no servers and cannot access, view, or obtain any of your data.

Data Export

Jiatu supports exporting all your data to a file stored in your iOS Files app. The export is triggered by you and remains fully under your control.

In-App Purchases

Jiatu's paid extensions (Storage Map, Floor Management) are fulfilled through Apple App Store In-App Purchases. Payment information is handled by Apple; we cannot access your payment details.

Minors

Jiatu does not collect personal information from anyone, including minors.

Policy Updates

If this policy is updated, a new version will be included with the app update.

Contact Us

If you have privacy-related questions, please contact: xingdachuan@outlook.com
